## Overview

MinimalDoc is a static site generator that runs entirely on your local machine. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data.

## Data Collection

MinimalDoc does not:

- Collect any personal information
- Send analytics or telemetry data
- Phone home to any servers
- Track your usage

## Generated Sites

The documentation sites you generate with MinimalDoc are entirely your own. MinimalDoc does not add any tracking scripts or analytics to your generated sites.

If you choose to add analytics or tracking to your generated sites, that is your responsibility.

## Third-Party Services

MinimalDoc may fetch OpenAPI specifications from remote URLs if you configure it to do so. This is initiated by you through the configuration file.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/studiowebux/minimaldoc).
